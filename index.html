Life Nugget

"For I know the plans I have for you," declares the Lord, "plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you hope and a future."

Jeremiah 29:11

The Bible's explanation is direct. We were made for connection with God, and something broke that connection. It calls this "sin." Not just doing bad things, but a deeper turning away from the source of life itself. The effects show up everywhere: in our relationships, our anxieties, our regrets, even in the world around us.

The problem is distance, not behaviour

The issue isn't that we haven't tried hard enough. Most of us have already tried that. The issue is a broken relationship, like a phone line that's been cut, not one that just has bad reception. Left on our own, we can't repair that kind of break.

What this means for you

The good news is that this isn't something you earn. You don't need to clean yourself up first. The invitation is simple: turn back toward God, trust that what Jesus did was enough, and let that relationship be restored.

What happens next is up to you

Maybe this is the first time you've heard it laid out simply. Maybe you grew up with it and drifted. Maybe you're skeptical, and that's fine too; questions are welcome.
